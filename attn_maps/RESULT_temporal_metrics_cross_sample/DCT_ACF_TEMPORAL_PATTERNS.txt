How to read DCT features:
  dct_low_frac  = fraction of temporal energy in slow/smooth changes
  dct_high_frac = fast jitter / step-to-step chatter
  dct_centroid  = energy-weighted "frequency" index (higher = more high-freq)
  dct_flatness  = white-like vs peaked spectrum
  acf1          = lag-1 autocorrelation (smoothness)
