STATISTICAL PROTOCOL
--------------------
Base object: last-layer text->patch attention A_t in R^{L x 256}, row-normalized.
Per-step mean-token entropy: H_tok(t) = (1/L) sum_i H(A_t[i,:]) / log(256)
  H(p) = -sum_j p_j log p_j  (normalized to [0,1])
Episode score: temporal mean  bar{H} = (1/T) sum_t H_tok(t)
Uncertainty: 95% bootstrap CI on bar{H} (resampling timesteps, B=5000).
Paired test: Wilcoxon signed-rank on {H_tok(t)} clean vs poison (same t).
CAVEAT: one episode; timesteps are temporally correlated — treat AUROC/
Wilcoxon as descriptive for this trajectory, not a multi-episode claim.
For paper-grade inference: repeat over N episodes and test on episode scores.
